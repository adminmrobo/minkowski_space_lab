Please create 1 image, aspect ratio 16:9, about 1600x900. Use the attached reference sprites for all characters.

COMMON STYLE (apply to every image):
16-bit SNES-era RPG pixel art, crisp pixel edges, limited palette, dithering allowed, no anti-aliased blur, no smooth anime shading, no 3D render. Bright daytime research lab: white walls and white floor tiles dominate the background, accents in soft teal, orange, navy and red. Characters must match the attached reference sprites exactly (same faces, hair, outfits), drawn as small chibi pixel sprites about 48–64 px tall in the scene:
- Professor: spiky black hair with one white streak, teal rectangular glasses, white lab coat over black vest and tie.
- Assistant Haru: long pink hair with a blue streak, two hair buns, lavender hoodie under a white lab coat.
- Fruit-fly researcher: navy round helmet with big red compound eyes and blue tubes, black insect body in a white lab coat, holds a mug or a magnifier.
- Abacus robot: beige boxy CRT-monitor head with a green pixel smile and a small antenna, wooden abacus belly, gray arms, tank treads.
- Intern Sia: long straight black hair, black maid-style lab dress with white frilled apron and gray bow.
- Intern Moa: long light-brown hair, white maid headband, mustard maid-style dress with white apron and red plaid bow.
Leave generous empty white space on the right third for title text. Do NOT draw any text, letters, numbers or symbols with writing.

IMAGE 1 — save as cover_index.png
A long white lab corridor seen from the side like an RPG dungeon hallway, seven doors in a row, a round pixel wall clock above each door showing different times. A dashed orange curve (hyperbola) floats beside the cone. Sia points at the glowing point, Moa holds a notebook and a stopwatch, the professor stands by door 1 holding a key, the abacus robot peeks from behind.
